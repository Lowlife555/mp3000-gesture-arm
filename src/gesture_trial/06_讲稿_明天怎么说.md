## 一、开场（30 秒，坐下来就说）

> "I spent last night building a small end-to-end pipeline from scratch, and I'd like to show you the result rather than describe it. I collected my own data with the laptop webcam, trained a small convolutional network, and then ran it live. Can I show you first and explain after?"
> 🇨🇳 我昨晚从零搭了一条很小的端到端链路，想先给你看结果，再解释。数据是我用笔记本摄像头自己采的，训练了一个小卷积网络，然后做实时推理。我可以先演示、后解释吗？

**为什么这样说**：先拿授权、先给画面 —— 视觉冲击比语言有效，而且他不会打断你。

## 二、演示（2 分钟，边做边说）

打开 `03_live_demo.py`，在方框里做三个手势。

> "There are three classes — fist, palm, and scissors. The box in the middle is the region of interest; everything outside it is thrown away, so the background does not matter much."
> 🇨🇳 一共三类：握拳、手掌、剪刀手。中间那个方框是感兴趣区域，框外全部丢掉，所以背景影响不大。

> "The three bars are the softmax probabilities. I am averaging the last ten frames, otherwise the prediction flickers — that is a simple temporal smoothing trick."
> 🇨🇳 三根条是 softmax 概率。我对最近十帧做了平均，否则预测会来回抖 —— 这是一个简单的时序平滑技巧。

> "The number at the bottom is inference time per frame, in milliseconds, on CPU."
> 🇨🇳 底下那个数字是 CPU 上每帧的推理耗时（毫秒）。

## 三、结构说明（1 分钟）

> "The network is deliberately small: three convolutional layers, then one fully connected layer, about two hundred thousand parameters — under one megabyte."
> 🇨🇳 网络故意做得很小：三层卷积 + 一个全连接层，约二十万个参数，不到 1 MB。

> "Convolution learns local templates. The first layer picks up edges; deeper layers combine edges into finger arrangements. Pooling halves the resolution so a small shift of the hand does not change the answer."
> 🇨🇳 卷积学的是局部模板。第一层抓到边缘，更深的层把边缘组合成手指的排布。池化把分辨率减半，这样手稍微移动不影响结果。

> "I used random rotation, zoom and translation during training — that is data augmentation. With only a few hundred images, without it the network would just memorise the training set."
> 🇨🇳 训练时我用了随机旋转、缩放、平移，这就是数据增强。只有几百张图，不加这个网络只会死记训练集。

## 四、主动交代局限（**这一段最加分，一定要说**）

> "I want to be explicit about what this is not. It is not a research result. It is three classes, a few hundred images, no hyper-parameter search and no cross-validation. The accuracy number here should not be read as a scientific claim."
> 🇨🇳 我想明确说清它不是什么。这不是研究成果。只有三类、几百张图，没做超参数搜索，也没做交叉验证。这里的准确率数字不能当作科学结论来读。

> "It is also sensitive to lighting. I mitigated that by cropping to the region of interest and by varying the lighting during collection, but it is not robust yet."
> 🇨🇳 它对光照也敏感。我靠裁剪 ROI、并在采集时刻意变换光照来缓解，但还不算稳健。

## 五、它和 YZ3 的关系（**转场到正题，这是今天真正的目的**）

> "I picked this direction on purpose. **Image recognition** is one of the five directions listed in your YZ3 brief, and it is also closest to your own area, so I thought you would be able to tell me where I am being naive."
> 🇨🇳 我是有意选这个方向的。**Image recognition** 是您 YZ3 题目里列出的五个方向之一，而且它离您的领域最近，所以我想您能直接指出我哪里想得太天真。

> "The part I actually find interesting is not the accuracy — it is the deployment path."
> 🇨🇳 我真正感兴趣的不是准确率，而是部署这条路径。

**然后展示量化结果**（`reports` 里的 `quantization.txt`）：

> "I converted the model to TensorFlow Lite and applied int8 post-training quantization. The file went from about 850 kilobytes to about 220 — roughly a factor of four — and accuracy did not drop. On a Cortex-M with one megabyte of flash and 128 kilobytes of RAM, that difference is the whole game."
> 🇨🇳 我把模型转成 TensorFlow Lite，做了 int8 训练后量化。文件从约 850 KB 降到约 220 KB，差不多四分之一，准确率没有掉。在只有 1 MB Flash、128 KB RAM 的 Cortex-M 上，这个差别就是决定性的。

> "I already have an STM32L476 board from ER3007, so the next step I would like to try is running the quantized model on the Cortex-M with CMSIS-NN, and measuring real latency and memory."
> 🇨🇳 我已经有 ER3007 那块 STM32L476 开发板，所以下一步我想试的是用 CMSIS-NN 把量化后的模型跑到 Cortex-M 上，实测延迟和内存。

## 六、结尾问他的三个问题（**必须问，不然白来**）

> "Three things I would like your view on —"
> 🇨🇳 有三件事想听您的意见 ——

**Q1（最重要）**
> "Is the deployment angle the right emphasis for a YZ3 project, or would you rather I went deeper on the modelling side?"
> 🇨🇳 对 YZ3 来说，"部署"这个侧重是对的吗？还是您更希望我在建模那边做深？

**Q2（拿真实资源）**
> "If I stay on this line, would you prefer I use a public dataset such as Google Speech Commands, or is it better to keep working with data I collect myself?"
> 🇨🇳 如果沿这条线做，您更希望我用 Google Speech Commands 这类公开数据集，还是继续用我自己采集的数据？

**Q3（对标传统方法，他一定能答）**
> "What would be a fair conventional baseline to compare against? I was thinking HOG features with an SVM — I would like the comparison to be honest rather than flattering to the network."
> 🇨🇳 什么才算公平的传统方法 baseline？我想到的是 HOG 特征 + SVM —— 我希望这个对比是诚实的，而不是故意让神经网络好看。

**为什么 Q3 这样问**：他 2013 年评估过神经网络、最后选了决策树。你主动提出跟传统方法对比，等于在说"我用你的标准要求自己"。
